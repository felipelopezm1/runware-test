# 06-sound: voices, foley and music

Every recorded line and every sound, all made on Runware.

**What goes here**

- `voices/`: every line of the script in that character's voice, `<line id>-<character>.mp3` (MiniMax Speech 2.8 by default, Fish S2.1 Pro where a line needs a cue such as a hiccup or a scream), with the casting notes and the audition takes
- `foley/`: the frogs' chorus, the worm's scream, rain, the lantern and steel, `<cue>.mp3` (Mirelo SFX 1.5)
- `music/`: the quiet low-string bed, 60 s, no lyrics (ACE-Step)

Where Seedance's own sound on a clip was better, it was kept and the foley for that shot skipped; the log says which.

**Where the files are:** what is committed is in this folder; everything else is in the [Drive folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1FWxTWA6g5YAU0IvnW3phteEQP4Db5o-Y) under `06-sound/`, which mirrors these folders. Nothing here points at a private machine.
