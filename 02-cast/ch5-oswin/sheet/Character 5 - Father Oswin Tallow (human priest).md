# CHARACTER 5: FATHER OSWIN TALLOW
### Human priest. Old, bearded, anxious. Heals, and deals water magic, which is no use at all against frogs.
Storyboard id `ch-oswin`. Prop `pr-crystal-staff`.
**Print:** `Creative Task Runware/Script/Characters/Character 5/IMG_8233.HEIC` (front), `IMG_8234` (right, staff to camera), `IMG_8235` (back, the cape), `IMG_8236` (left three-quarter). The print: an elderly man with a long beard, a flat round cap, a long belted robe with a round medallion on the chest, a long cape to the boots, a tall staff topped with a faceted crystal in the right hand, the left hand raised high and open: casting.

## Who he is
**Class and background:** Cleric (Tempest Domain, the sea-and-rain church). Background: Acolyte, a librarian priest of seventy who has never been this far from a desk.
**Personality:** anxious, bookish, earnest, devout, apologises constantly, and braver than he thinks. He keeps insisting the water spells should work ("they're amphibians, they should... I don't understand") while the frogs enjoy the bath. He is the party's second healer and knows he is the second. He prays under his breath; everyone can hear it. He and Borrin are friends: the dwarf is the only one who tells him he did well.
**Wants:** to be useful. **Fears:** the frogs, specifically, and failing Vel generally.
**Voice:** light, reedy, old tenor, quick, breathy, stammers under stress.
**Signature gesture:** the left hand up, palm open, before every spell (the print's pose); he wipes his beard with the back of it after.

When he casts he raises his open left hand high and clear water gathers out of the air around it and around the crystal with a blue-white glow. He holds his shoulders high and blinks often.

## Magic, in detail
- **Water magic, how it looks**: clear water gathers out of the air around his raised LEFT hand and the crystal, glowing blue-white, then launches as a fist-thick jet or a wave 2 m wide. On the frogs it does nothing but wash them and make them shinier; one sits in the puddle and croaks at him. On the worm (3C) a full wave slaps mud off its flank and buys Vessa a second.
- **Healing**: a blue-white trickle from the crystal, slower and thinner than Sylwen's green pour.
- The staff is never swung as a weapon. No mace, no book on a chain.

## How he moves and fights
Behind the line, next to Sylwen, back to a wall when he can. Casts with his whole body, left hand high. In scene 2 the frogs charge him (`Scene 3 Actions/IMG_0330` has two red arcs from the floor frogs to his base and a red cross where one dies at his feet; in `Scene 2 Actions/IMG_0337` his red arc is his jet at a frog).
