# CHARACTER 3: DAX VORRUNE
### Half-githyanki barbarian. Black complexion. Sword broken clean through the middle. Loves to jump; slips when he lands.
Storyboard id `ch-dax`. Prop `pr-halfblade`.
**Print:** `Creative Task Runware/Script/Characters/Character 3/IMG_8224.HEIC` (front), `IMG_8225` (left), `IMG_8226` (back), `IMG_8227` (right). The print: a very muscular bare-chested man in a fur loincloth with a round buckle, a headband, shoulder-length hair, bracers and fur-topped boots, both hands on a sword hilt whose blade is broken short, braced in a two-handed guard. (The print's stub is shorter than Felipe's brief; the brief wins: the blade is broken through the MIDDLE, so half of it remains.)

## Who he is
**Class and background:** Barbarian (Path of the Zealot: he believes every leap is a prayer). Background: Gladiator and entertainer, raised in a pit arena where the crowd loved the jump more than the kill.
**Personality:** a showman. Cocky, generous, impossible to insult, counts his jumps aloud, treats every landing as a performance even when his fur-topped boots skid out from under him, and laughs first at himself. He adores Vessa's grumbling and Borrin's songs. He is the only one who finds Vel funny and the only one she lets.
**Wants:** the perfect landing. **Fears:** being left behind.
**Voice:** bright quick baritone, fast talker, ends lines with a grin you can hear.
**Signature gesture:** two bounces on his toes before any jump; a two-handed flourish of the Halfblade after he lands, even when he is on his back.

Two-handed grip (the print's). He uses it like a heavy cleaver and a lever: downward chops from a jump, a flat-end jab, and he plants it in the ground or in the worm to stop a skid. It is NOT Vessa's sword: hers is broken at the tip, his in the middle.

## How he moves and fights
Vertical. Two bounces, a jump 2 to 3 m high, a chop on the way down, a landing that skids half a metre with arms out for balance, then a flourish. In scene 2 he leaps from the top of the stairs over the barrels into the corridor (the big blue arc in `Scene 2 Actions/IMG_0338`). In scene 3 the worm notices him first (the green line from its head to him in `Scene 3 Actions/IMG_0330`); his jump from the rubble onto the worm's head with the Halfblade driven in is the blow that opens its mouth for Mordecai; he slides off the slick skin into the mud.
