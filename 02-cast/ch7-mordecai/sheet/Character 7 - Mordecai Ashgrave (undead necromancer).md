# CHARACTER 7: MORDECAI ASHGRAVE
### Undead. Necromancer. Vel's rival for the title of strongest. Deals the final blow to the worm.
Storyboard id `ch-mordecai`.
**Print:** `Creative Task Runware/Script/Characters/Character 7/IMG_8241.HEIC` (front), `IMG_8242` (right), `IMG_8243` (back), `IMG_8244` (left). The print is a tall skull-faced figure in a horned helmet whose two horns meet in a ring with a ball on top, layered armour with small skulls, a long tabard, no weapon, both arms raised high and wide: that V of raised arms is his casting pose and the pose of the kill shot.

## Who he is
**Class and background:** Wizard (School of Necromancy), himself undead: a scholar who refused to stop. Background: Sage.
**Personality:** theatrical, vain, sardonic, delighted by combat, a connoisseur of other people's deaths. He keeps score with Vel out loud, always in his own favour. He is fond of the party the way a collector is fond of a set. He finds the frogs beneath him and the worm magnificent.
**Wants:** to be acknowledged as the strongest, by Vel, once. **Fears:** boredom.
**Voice:** dry resonant baritone with a rasp like dry leaves, a faint hollow echo as if from a jar. Relishes consonants. Laughs on an inhale.
**Signature gesture:** both arms raised wide, palms open, green flame gathering in each hand before he casts.

## Magic, in detail
- **Soul flame**: acid-green, steady, smokeless, throwing hard green light over everything within 5 m when it is up. Small spells are a ring of fire swept out along the floor with one arm; the kill (3E) is a single thick beam from both raised hands into the worm's open mouth, with his arms in the print's wide V.
- No staff, no dagger, no scythe: his hands are the weapon in every shot.

## How he moves and fights
Slow until he is not. Walks as if the floor is beneath him by permission. Lets the frogs come to him and burns them in a ring. Against the worm he waits for the mouth to open (he says so) and takes the kill at the last possible moment, standing on the rubble above the mud, tabard streaming in the rain, both arms raised wide.
