# CHARACTER 1: VESSA ROWANBLOOD
### Human barbarian. Red hair. A greatsword snapped at the tip that she refuses to replace, and her mother's shield. Young, rugged, good-looking.
Storyboard id `ch-vessa`. Props `pr-gryma`, `pr-shield`.
**Print:** `Creative Task Runware/Script/Characters/Character 1/IMG_8216.HEIC` (front), `IMG_8217` (right, shield to camera), `IMG_8218` (back, the bent blade tip clear), `IMG_8219` (left); scale shots `Size from afar/IMG_8212` to `8215`. The print: sword raised straight overhead in the right hand with its blade broken at the top third, a heater shield with a knot emblem on the left arm, scale cuirass with rounded pauldrons, a skirt of hanging tassets over bare thighs, bracers, fur-topped knee boots, long hair past the shoulders.

## Who she is
**Class and background:** Barbarian (Path of the Berserker). Background: Outlander, from the highland reaver clans.
**Personality:** hot-blooded, warm, loud, loyal to the point of stupidity, sentimental about exactly two objects: her mother's greatsword Gryma, its last third snapped off in the fight that killed her mother, which she has fought with ever since and calls "the two thirds of her that still bite", and her mother's shield. She laughs in fights, swears cheerfully, teases Dax, protects Oswin without being asked, and obeys Vel because Vel has never been wrong.
**Wants:** a fight worth the sword. **Fears:** small rooms.
**Voice:** warm low alto with a rasp, highland lilt, laughs mid-sentence.
**Signature gesture:** kisses the broken blade before a charge; bangs the sword on the shield rim to call a fight.

## Weapons, in detail
- **GRYMA**: a greatsword 1.3 m long when whole; the top third of the blade is gone, so what remains is 80 cm of bright steel ending in a jagged, slightly bent break (the print's kinked tip reads as this break). Wide steel crossguard, 30 cm grip wrapped in dark brown leather, heavy bronze ram's-head pommel. She swings it one-handed, overhead and in wide arcs, and hammers with the pommel. The break is at the TIP: Dax's sword is broken in the MIDDLE. Both breaks must read clearly whenever the two swords share a frame.
- **The shield**: a heater shield, 60 cm tall, oak boards rimmed in grey steel, face painted dark red, a raised bronze triple-knot (triskelion) emblem. Left arm. She blocks tongues and toads with it and in scene 3 goes down behind it when the coil sweeps her.

## How she moves and fights
Charges. Closes distance in three steps, plants her feet, sword overhead and down, shield up. Takes hits rather than dodging. In scene 2 a toad knocks her flat on her back among the frogs (`Scene 2/IMG_8130`, `Scene 2 Actions/IMG_0336`) and she laughs getting up. In scene 3 the coil's sweep puts her on her back behind the shield (3C); she gets up and keeps swinging.
