### Elf healer. Keeps the party alive through the worm fight, even from inside its coil. The still point of the frame.
Storyboard id `ch-sylwen`.

The print: a slim woman in a long sleeveless belted gown with a front slit, bare feet, long straight hair to the shoulder blades, arms folded low with the hands clasped, head slightly down, no weapon. In the Scene 3 build her print lies inside the worm's coil (`Scene 3/IMG_8141` to `8143`, `8160` to `8165`): that is the beat where the coil takes her.

## Who she is
**Class and background:** Druid (Circle of Dreams), a healer of the old green kind. Background: Hermit, three hundred years in a forest that is now a road.
**Personality:** calm, dry, precise, unhurried. Short sentences. She notices everything and says one tenth of it. Mothering without sentiment: she will close your wound and tell you it was your own fault in the same breath. She finds the rivalry between Vel and Mordecai childish and says so once. She does not hurry even when she is being crushed.
**Wants:** everyone breathing at the end. **Fears:** nothing she will admit to.
**Voice:** soft, even, slightly husky, no exclamations ever.
**Signature gesture:** her hands unclasp from her belly and open, palms up, and the green light pools in them.

- Soft green light, not lightning, not sparkles: a slow pour from her open palms into the wound, which closes over in two seconds leaving a pink line. Her face does not change. Vessa's forearm (2B, 3C), Oswin's hand (2B), Borrin's hands on the axe (3C), Dax after the slip (3E), from inside the coil for part of it.
- She never fights. When a frog comes at her she steps aside and it passes.

## How she moves and fights
Stays behind Vel's line and moves only to where someone is bleeding. In scene 3 the worm's coil closes round her (3C) with her arms free above it; she heals Vessa through the gap without raising her voice; Vel pulls her out as the coil loosens (3D); she kneels by Dax (3E) and does not look up at the kill.
