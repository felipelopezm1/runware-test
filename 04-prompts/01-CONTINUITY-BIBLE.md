# CONTINUITY BIBLE
### The fixed words and images every prompt in this production repeats

This file is the law for appearance and performance across all three scenes and every take. A **LOCK BLOCK** is pasted into a prompt exactly as written here. The reference order is fixed so `[Image1]`, `[Image2]`... mean the same thing in every Seedance call. The **state ladder** says how each character looks at each point in the story, so wounds, mud and rain accumulate and never reset. The source of every silhouette, pose and costume piece is the printed mini photographed in `Creative Task Runware/Script/Characters/` (paths in `02-ASSET-PATHS.md`); the words below turn those prints into people.

Storyboard ids: `ch-vel`, `ch-mordecai`, `ch-vessa`, `ch-dax`, `ch-borrin`, `ch-sylwen`, `ch-oswin`, `ch-mob-frogs`, `ch-mob-worm`; `lo-storeroom`, `lo-corridor`, `lo-roofless-hall`; props `pr-needle` (Vel's rapier), `pr-acid-pots`, `pr-gryma` (Vessa's sword, broken at the tip), `pr-shield`, `pr-halfblade` (Dax's sword, broken in the middle), `pr-axe` (Borrin's axe with the lantern), `pr-flask`, `pr-crystal-staff` (Oswin's).

## 1. The look block (goes first in every prompt)

> LOOK: hyper-realistic live-action fantasy, photoreal cinema. Real human skin with pores and sweat, real wool, leather, iron and wet stone. Anamorphic character, 16:9. Practical light only: lantern amber and flame against cold slate-blue shadow, light falling off into true black. Shallow focus on faces, deep focus on wide shots. Film grain fine, no bloom, no haze unless written. Nothing reads as a toy, miniature, 3D print, clay, render or game engine. No text, no watermark, no logo.

**Avoid list (Seedance takes no negative prompt, so describe the absence as the hold line):** "The frame holds exactly N people. Every character keeps the same face, hair, skin, costume and weapon as in the references. No extra limbs, no duplicated characters, no floating weapons, no modern objects, no text."

### lo-storeroom: the storeroom under the stairs (scene 1; build: `Scenes/Scene 1/IMG_8085` to `8114`)
> STOREROOM: a rectangular vaulted cellar of dark grey ashlar stone, 8 m by 6 m, 5 m to the apex of a ribbed barrel vault, the mortar black with damp, moss and pale lichen in the joints, water running in thin lines down the walls. Along the back-left corner three stacked wooden crates and a slumped sack; at the front left a cluster of three iron-banded barrels and a sack, one barrel's lid off and full of black water. From the floor at the front a straight stone staircase 1.5 m wide climbs along the right wall to a dark doorway in the back-right corner, its steps worn hollow and slick, two more crates and a fallen block at its top. ### lo-corridor: the corridor (scene 2; build: `Scenes/Scene 2/IMG_8119` to `8133`)
> CORRIDOR: a long barrel-vaulted dungeon passage 3 m wide and 4 m high, 30 m long, of the same dark grey ashlar, its far end the top of the storeroom's staircase and its near end an open archway with cold grey light beyond. The floor is flagged and puddled ankle deep, the walls weep. Along the left wall a long low stone bench; on the right wall a barred window niche; a fallen stone slab near the far end; a crate and a barrel in a side opening near the stairs; iron sconces every 6 m holding dead torches; chains hanging from the vault. Lantern light moves with the party; a cold grey-blue glow grows from the near archway.

### lo-roofless-hall: the roofless hall of the fallen castle (scene 3; build: `Scenes/Scene 3/IMG_0112`, `8139` to `8180`)
> ROOFLESS HALL: the great hall of a ruined castle with its roof long gone, open to a cold moonless night sky thick with stars; high broken walls of pale weathered limestone on three sides, taller at the back, their jagged tops against the stars, the fourth side the dark archway the party enters by. In the centre a wide mound of fallen rubble and masonry sunk in a patch of black churned mud 20 m across, bubbling, steaming, strewn with old bones, broken barrels and rotten cloth, a smell of death made visible as a faint haze; pale crescent plates of sloughed skin scattered over everything. A long stone bench to one side with frogs on it, a barred window niche and a fallen slab pushed against the walls, more barrels and crates in the corners, long wet grass and nettles in the cracks. The light is starlight, the dwarf's lantern, Mordecai's green flame and later lightning. From shot 3D, cold rain falls in sheets, the mud shines, and water runs off the walls.

## 7. Camera and performance rules carried into every clip prompt

- Action first, then who, then where, then camera, then light. One main action a shot. At most two faces in close-up.
- Give the timeline in seconds inside the prompt ("0 to 2 s: ... 2 to 5 s: ...") because Seedance cuts inside one clip.
- Name the camera once: lens, angle, move. Never two moves.
- Say what stays still so the model does not invent motion.
- Say the sound, and write every spoken line in quotes with the speaker's name and a delivery note, so Seedance performs it.
- The party moves away from the camera and up in scene 1 (to the stairs), toward and past the camera in scene 2 (down the corridor), and into the frame from the left in scene 3; a shot says so when it departs from this.
- One resolution for the whole film: Seedance 2.5 at 4K when its schema offers it, else 1080p for every shot and one Topaz pass at the end; never mix.
- One pinned model per job: Nano Banana Pro (`google:4@2`) for every frame and panel, Seedance 2.5 (`bytedance:seedance@2.5`) for every clip, the same `seed` on a shot's retakes where the schema allows one.
