# 10-b-roll: the build on camera

Felipe's own footage of printing, sculpting and painting the minis and building the sets. Listed in the log for the making-of; never used as a model input.

**What goes here**

- `starting/`: the first prints and the first clay (15 files)
- `scene-2/`, `scene-3/`: the builds of the corridor and the hall (4 and 7 files)
- `molding-and-sculpting/`, `waking-and-finishing/`: the sculpting and finishing passes
- `posters/`: a poster frame of every clip

**Where the files are:** what is committed is in this folder; everything else is in the [Drive folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1FWxTWA6g5YAU0IvnW3phteEQP4Db5o-Y) under `10-b-roll/`, which mirrors these folders. Nothing here points at a private machine.

**Status:** 27 clips and photos exist (about 900 MB of phone video); they go to Drive, with the posters here
