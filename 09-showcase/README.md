# 09-showcase: what goes out

The three things the brief asks for beside the film.

**What goes here**

- `social-post.md`: a builder's post (X or LinkedIn) with the frames it would carry
- `website-piece.md`: an outline for a Runware website piece ("seven minis and a worm: a 60 s film on Seedance 2.5 through one API") with the key points, the pipeline and the costs
- `walkthrough/`: the screen recording of up to 20 minutes walking through the thinking and the process
- `videos/`: the end videos, finished on 20 November 2026: `how-it-was-done-1080p.mp4` (the 15-minute making-of, with its sound stems), `comparison-reel-1080p.mp4` and `comparison-reel-1080p-cycle.mp4` (clay beside take beside finish, shot by shot, and the same as a loop); the Titanium explainer is still to come. Too big for Git, so they are on Drive under `09-showcase/videos/` and on the [GitHub release](https://github.com/felipelopezm1/runware-test/releases/tag/v1.0)

Both texts are drafted by the agents from the log and rewritten by hand before anything is published.
