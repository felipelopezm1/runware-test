## LOCK BLOCK (paste unchanged)
> ROOFLESS HALL: the great hall of a ruined castle with its roof long gone, open to a cold moonless night sky thick with stars; high broken walls of pale weathered limestone on three sides, taller at the back, their jagged tops against the stars, the fourth side the dark archway the party enters by. In the centre a wide mound of fallen rubble and masonry sunk in a patch of black churned mud 20 m across, bubbling, steaming, strewn with old bones, broken barrels and rotten cloth, a smell of death made visible as a faint haze; pale crescent plates of sloughed skin scattered over everything. A long stone bench to one side with frogs on it, a barred window niche and a fallen slab pushed against the walls, more barrels and crates in the corners, long wet grass and nettles in the cracks. The light is starlight, the dwarf's lantern, Mordecai's green flame and later lightning. From shot 3D, cold rain falls in sheets, the mud shines, and water runs off the walls.

## Layout (from the build)
The party enters through the archway at the LEFT of the master view and fans out onto broken flagstones. The bench with the last frogs is centre, between the party and the mud. The rubble mound is centre right with the worm coiled twice around it, its head raised on the right; barrels sit inside the coil. The back wall is the tallest; its fallen blocks make a stepped platform at the back right: Dax's launch point and Mordecai's perch. The sky is a third of every wide frame: thick stars, the Milky Way, no moon. The coil is where Sylwen is caught (`IMG_8141` to `8143`, `8160` to `8165`).

## Materials and light words
Pale weathered limestone, blotched grey and ochre with lichen, black water stains. Long wet grass, nettles, broken flags. The mud: black, glossy, bubbling, bones and barrel staves half sunk, pale crescent plates everywhere, a brown haze above. Starlight: cold blue-white, enough to read the walls. Lantern amber at the mud's edge, Mordecai's acid green, and from 3D: rain in sheets backlit by the green flame, mud shining, water running off everything, one lightning flash in 3E on the beam.

## Sound
Wind over the walls, grass, the mud's slow bubbling, the lantern, a few frogs; then the scream; then rain building from a patter to a roar; thunder once on the kill; then rain only, and breathing.
