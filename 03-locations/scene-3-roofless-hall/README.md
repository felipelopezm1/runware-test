# Scene 3: the roofless hall

The fallen castle's great hall, 40 m by 25 m, open to a cold starry night, a 20 m disc of reeking mud with the rubble mound the worm is coiled round, a stepped platform of fallen blocks at the back right.

**What goes here**

- `environment.md`: `Scene 3 - Environment.md` from the prompt pack
- `build-photos/`: 35 photos of the clay build
- `action-maps/`: 4 action maps drawn over the build photos

- `plates/`: `lo-roofless-hall-1.png`, `lo-roofless-hall-2.png`, their layout overlays and the prompts that drew them
