## LOCK BLOCK (paste unchanged)
> CORRIDOR: a long barrel-vaulted dungeon passage 3 m wide and 4 m high, 30 m long, of the same dark grey ashlar, its far end the top of the storeroom's staircase and its near end an open archway with cold grey light beyond. The floor is flagged and puddled ankle deep, the walls weep. Along the left wall a long low stone bench; on the right wall a barred window niche; a fallen stone slab near the far end; a crate and a barrel in a side opening near the stairs; iron sconces every 6 m holding dead torches; chains hanging from the vault. Lantern light moves with the party; a cold grey-blue glow grows from the near archway.

## Layout (from the build)
The party comes out of the stair doorway at the FAR end and advances toward the camera and the near archway (the long blue arrow in `IMG_0333`). Borrin leads, wading through frogs (`IMG_0334`, `0336`, `0339`: his kills); Vel and Vessa behind him; Sylwen near the stairs; Dax and Mordecai hold the top of the stairs with the barrels behind them (`IMG_8128`), Dax leaps from there over the barrels (`IMG_0338`, the big blue arc) and Mordecai throws his fire down the corridor (the red spiral and arc in the same map). Vessa is knocked onto her back among the frogs (`IMG_8130`, `IMG_0336`). Twenty frogs on the floor, two on the bench; toads behind them. At the end the frogs run PAST the party back toward the stairs.

## Materials and light words
Same ashlar, wetter: water runs in sheets down the walls and the floor is one shallow puddle, so every light doubles in reflection. Rust streaks under each sconce. The bench is a single worn slab; the niche has three iron bars. Light: Borrin's lantern leading (warm, moving), Mordecai's green fire from the stairs behind throwing hard green down the right wall, the cold grey glow from the near archway growing as the party advances. Vel's acid pot: a green-white flash and smoke.

## Sound
The frog chorus very close, echoing down the passage; splashing feet; steel; the hiss of acid and the roar of green fire; water running; at the end, the chorus retreating and a cold wind from the archway.
