# Screen recordings

The outside recording of the desktop while the quest ran, made so that the whole process can be watched back and explored: the Loom with its spiders, the storyboard editor, the Blender windows, the quotes and the charges.

**What goes here**

- `loom-YYYYmmdd-HHMM.mp4`: 10-minute segments, 10 fps, H.264, about 85 MB each
- `shots/shot-YYYYmmdd-HHMMSS.png`: one full-screen shot a minute
- `record.sh`, `shots.sh`, `README.md`: the scripts and their start and stop log

**Comes from** (on the laptop)

- `/home/felipe-lopez/titanium/.hephaestus/storyboards/is-this-all-on-runware/making-of/recordings/`
