`history/10.json`) is the copy faithful to the pack, shot for shot; rev 15 is the shooting board: 16 takes, 62.0 s on
screen, 72 s generated. Rev 15 adds three changes on the coordinator's word (C5, C6, D1). Nothing was generated and
nothing was spent.

The owner's licence for this: "Where a shot ... works better another way ... reshape a shot, split or merge takes ...
Keep three things fixed: the story and its ending, each character's identity (the LOCK BLOCKS), and the honesty of
the log." The three fixed things are untouched: `tools/director/verify_shooting.py` finds every LOCK BLOCK, location
block, the LOOK, the three SCENE blocks, WORLD, the hold line and all 40 lines' words identical to the pack
(`notes/shooting-verify.txt`). Every row below comes from `notes/director-proposals.md` (named in the last column) or
from the coordinator's instructions for this pass; the rows marked *pass* are this pass's own decisions, each with
its reason.

(`Scene N - Shot list.md`), `SCRIPT.md`, the bible (`01-CONTINUITY-BIBLE.md`) and the ambiances file. The pack was
