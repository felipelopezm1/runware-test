## Verdict on the earlier, unverified reading

1. **"The TALL wall is a curved wall round the end behind the worm (the right-hand end of the IMG_0112 master view), not a back wall behind the whole hall."** **Confirmed.** It is an apse from (+12.9, -10.0) through the crown at (+20, 0) to (+12.3, +12.6). Its -Z end is cut off square at full height; its +Z end steps down into the low +Z wall. Seen in `0111`, `8139`, `8141` to `8143`, `8147` to `8152`, the clip; its absence along -Z in `0112` and `8144`.
2. **"The stepped blocks Dax launches from and Mordecai stands on are at the right-hand end on the camera's side, not the back right."** **Confirmed as to place, with two caveats.** The only stepped stone is the curved wall's +Z end at (+9 to +12.5, +11 to +12.6), at the master view's right-hand end on the camera's side. A second, lower stepped pile (crate, barrel, loose blocks) stands on the same side at (-1.4, +10.5). But no photo or map puts Dax or Mordecai on either: `0330` sends Dax from the party to the coil and `0329` fires Mordecai's beam from the party's place on the floor (rows 18 and 19).
3. **"The worm makes about 1.3 turns round the mound, not two."** **Confirmed for the flat coil, corrected for the later photos.** Traced ring by ring on `0111`, the coil sweeps about 413 degrees, about 1.15 turns (1.1 to 1.3 within the error), clockwise from tail to head seen from above; that is the state of every 3A and 3B photo. From `8159` to `8170` the owner rewound it into a stacked two-tier coil of about two turns with the caught figure in the upper ring: the 3C and 3D state, where "coiled twice" holds.
4. **"If it holds, the words 'back wall' and 'back right' in shots 3A, 3D and 3E describe a layout the build does not have."** **Partly.** The build has no tall wall along the master view's back (-Z) and nothing at (+X, -Z): there axes.md is wrong and must change. But 3A, 3D and 3E are seen from the party's side, looking +X, where back is the curved end wall and back right is its stepped +Z end, and the build has both. The words can stay if anchored to that view ("the curved end wall behind the worm"; "the stepped end of that wall, right of the worm as the party sees it"). What the build and maps contradict in those shots is who stands on the blocks (rows 18 and 19), not where the blocks are.
