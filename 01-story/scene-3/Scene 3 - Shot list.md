**Art direction:** the sky is a character: a third of every wide frame, star-thick, moonless, enormous after the dungeon, seen through the broken tops of the walls. Light sources: starlight (cold), the lantern (amber, dying), Mordecai's flame (green, growing), lightning once. Rain from 3D turns every surface to glass. Mud on everyone accumulates by the state ladder. The worm is always partly under the mud and coiled round the mound. The final tableau is still, symmetrical and quiet after thirty seconds of noise.

---

## 3A. Nobody on the mud (5 s)
**Frame (references `IMG_8139`, `IMG_0112`, the hall plate, the prints):** from behind the party at the archway, low, the seven fanning out onto broken flagstones, the stone bench with a few frogs ahead at centre, beyond it the mound of rubble and black mud with a vast pale coil half sunk round it, the fallen keep blocks at the back right, the stars filling the top third through the broken walls.
**SHOT body:**
```
0 to 3 s: from behind the party, the camera rises slowly from knee height to above their heads as they step out of the dark archway into the roofless great hall of a ruined castle under a vast moonless sky thick with stars. High broken limestone walls on three sides, a long stone bench ahead with the last few frogs on it, fallen blocks of the keep at the back right, long wet grass, low mist, and in the centre a mound of rubble sunk in a wide patch of black mud, glossy, bubbling slowly, with old bones and broken barrels half sunk in it, a brown haze above it, and something huge and pale coiled round the mound under the muck. VEL raises one hand and everyone stops; she says (quiet): "Nobody on the mud." DAX bounces twice on his toes and says (grinning): "Define 'on'." 3 to 5 s: the frogs on the bench leap off it and scatter toward the mound; the coil heaves under the mud and a long ripple runs across it toward BORRIN's lantern at the mud's edge. SYLWEN says (even): "Borrin. Step back." Camera: 24 mm, a smooth crane rise, deep focus, the party's backs in silhouette against the lantern and the mud. Light: starlight on the walls, the amber lantern, the green flame at the back of the group. Sound: wind over the walls, grass, the mud's slow bubbling, the lantern, frogs scattering, no music.
```

## 3B. The scream (5 s) — Borrin's POV
**Frame (references `IMG_8146`, `IMG_8156`, Mob 2's prints, the hall plate):** from Borrin's height at the mud's edge, the lantern on his axe in the lower left of the frame, the mound and the coil filling the lower half, the coil beginning to rise, stars above the back wall.
**SHOT body:**
```
0 to 1 s: from BORRIN's point of view at the edge of the mud, the lantern hanging lit from his axe in the lower left of the frame, the pale coil round the rubble mound heaving at centre. 1 to 3 s: the mud explodes upward and THE WORM rears out of its coil, a 2 m thick body of translucent pink-grey-brown segmented skin slick with mucus and caked in black mud rising straight up eight metres, pale crescent plates of skin sloughing off it, the camera tilting up with it, shaking, until its blind rounded head is against the stars; the head peels open into a round ringed mouth and it SCREAMS, a wet piercing bubbling shriek; mud rains down on the lens. 3 to 5 s: at the right edge OSWIN's left hand comes up high wrapped in water and blue-white light and he prays fast (breathless, old): "Lord of the deep water, Lord of the deep water, Lord of the..." At the left edge MORDECAI spreads both arms wide with green flame blazing in each palm and says (thrilled, over the scream): "Now THAT is worth getting up for." VEL's arm sweeps through the frame throwing a clay pot that bursts on the worm's flank in an acid-green hiss and white smoke, and she shouts: "Flanks! Vessa, the collar!" Camera: 28 mm, low, one tilt up with the eruption, handheld shake, the lantern always in frame. Light: lantern amber from below, green from the left, starlight, the acid flash. Sound: the mud bursting, the scream with a sub-bass body, mud hitting stone and glass, plates of skin slapping down, the pot's hiss, shouted voices under the scream.
```

## 3C. It's soft (5 s)
**Frame (references `IMG_8141`, `IMG_8144`, Vessa's and Sylwen's prints, Mob 2):** a handheld medium on Vessa hacking at the worm's pale swollen collar with her broken-tipped sword, shield up, mud running over her; the coil curving behind her with Sylwen standing inside its loop; the back wall and stars behind.
**SHOT body:**
```
0 to 2 s: handheld, close on VESSA as she hacks at the worm's swollen pale collar with her broken-tipped sword, her red shield up, mud and mucus sliding over her bare tattooed arms; she laughs through her teeth and says: "It's SOFT. Why is it SOFT." The coil sweeps the ground behind her: it slams her flat on her back behind her shield in the black mud, and closes round SYLWEN to the waist where she stands, her arms free above it, her face unchanged. 2 to 3.5 s: a wave of clear water crashes across the frame from the left and slaps a sheet of mud off the worm's flank; OSWIN, half in frame at the left with his left hand high and glowing, says (astonished): "It worked. It WORKED." 3.5 to 5 s: SYLWEN, held in the coil, bends down to VESSA's bleeding left forearm beside her, lays two glowing green palms on it, the wound closing to a pink line, and says (dry, not looking at the worm): "Hold still. Yes, it's soft. Hold still." The body coils on behind them, half under the mud. Camera: 50 mm, eye level, handheld follow that stays with Vessa from the collar to the ground and then holds on the two women. Light: starlight, lantern amber from the right, green flame flaring behind the worm, Sylwen's soft green pour. Sound: the worm's wet movement, the slam, the splash, the wave, Oswin's voice, Sylwen's quiet voice close to the microphone.
```

## 3D. Eight (6 s) — over Vessa's shoulder, the rain begins
**Frame (references `IMG_8146`, `IMG_8160`, Dax's print mid-leap pose, the hall plate in rain):** hero-low from behind Vessa's shoulder as she pushes up out of the mud: Dax at the top of the fallen blocks back right mid-launch, the worm's head rearing toward him, Sylwen in the coil at the left, the first streaks of rain against the green flame, the stars above.
**SHOT body:**
```
0 to 1 s: low, from behind VESSA's shoulder as she pushes herself up out of the mud behind her shield, the camera arcs right to follow DAX sprinting up the fallen blocks at the back of the hall. 1 to 3 s: the sky opens: cold rain falls in sheets, backlit green by Mordecai's flame, and DAX launches from the top block, both hands on his square-ended half sword, three metres into the air, shouting (bright): "EIGHT!" He lands on the worm's blind rearing head and drives the half blade in to the crossguard; the worm SCREAMS and whips upright; his fur boots slip on the slick skin and he slides down its side into the mud. 3 to 6 s: the coil round SYLWEN loosens as the body writhes. VESSA scrambles forward shouting (raw): "DAX!" VEL, already in the mud up to her knees, grabs SYLWEN under both arms and hauls her up and out of the loop of the coil, saying through her teeth, toward Dax: "I said. Nobody. On the mud." Rain hammers the mud flat and runs off everyone. Camera: 35 mm, hero-low from waist height behind Vessa, one smooth arc to the right following Dax from the blocks to the head to the mud, no cut. Light: green flame through the rain, starlight fading behind cloud, the lantern guttering, the mud shining. Sound: rain rising from a patter to a roar, the scream, the blade going in, the slide and the splash, three shouted voices.
```

## 3E. This one is mine (5 s) — Vel's POV from the mud
**Frame (references `IMG_8156`, Mordecai's print with the arms raised, Mob 2 mouth-open panel):** low angle from the mud looking up: Mordecai on the highest fallen block, tabard streaming in the rain, both arms raised wide with green flame blazing in each hand; beyond and above him the worm's head, mouth wide open and ringed; stars and rain.
**SHOT body:**
```
0 to 2 s: from VEL's point of view kneeling in the mud, the camera looks up through sheets of rain at MORDECAI standing on the highest fallen block, his plum tabard streaming, both arms raised high and wide with acid-green flame blazing in each skeletal hand; above and beyond him THE WORM rears to its full height and SCREAMS with its round mouth peeled wide open, rings of pale ridges leading into black. He says (grandly, to the camera): "Watch closely, Captain. This one is mine." 2 to 3.5 s: a thick beam of acid-green soul-flame fires from both his hands straight down the worm's throat; the camera snap-zooms onto his skull and horned helmet and settles; one bolt of lightning splits the sky behind the walls and thunder cracks; the worm's body spasms, the scream cuts off, and the head falls away out of the top of the frame onto the mound. 3.5 to 5 s: at the bottom edge of the frame VEL's gloved hands set SYLWEN down in the mud beside DAX, who lies winded and grinning; she says (flat, not looking up): "Assist." MORDECAI, on his block, turns his skull toward her and says (outraged, delighted): "It is NOT an assist." Camera: 85 mm, low angle looking up, static, then one snap zoom onto the skull that settles. Light: the green beam lighting the rain and the whole hall green, the lightning white, the lantern a tiny amber ember. Sound: the scream, the beam's roar, thunder, the body falling into mud, rain, two voices.
```

## 3F. Is this all on Runware? (4 s) — smash cut
**Frame (references `IMG_0111`, `IMG_8168`, the sky plate, all seven prints):** a low wide tableau from the mud: the seven of them in a loose line, Vel standing with her rapier point down, Mordecai with his skull tilted up, Vessa with an arm round Dax who sits in the mud, Borrin with the faint ember of the lantern on his axe and his flask, Sylwen standing with her hands clasped, Oswin wringing out his beard; the dead worm's pale body slumped over the mound behind them; all looking up; the sky two thirds of the frame through the broken walls, thick with stars, the rain thinning; the mud still and steaming.
**SHOT body:**
```
0 to 3 s: a locked-off, low, wide tableau from the edge of the still mud, the dead worm's pale body slumped over the rubble mound behind. The seven of them, soaked and filthy, stand and sit in a loose line looking up at the enormous starry sky through the roofless walls: VEL standing, rain running off her chin, rapier point down; MORDECAI with his skull tilted up like a man warming his face in the sun, the flame out; VESSA with an arm around DAX, who sits in the mud grinning, winded; BORRIN with the lantern a faint ember on his axe and his flask in his other hand; SYLWEN standing with her hands clasped at her belly again; OSWIN wringing out his long white beard with both hands. Nobody speaks. The rain thins to a drizzle; the mud steams. 3 to 4 s: BORRIN raises the flask to the stars and says (quiet, slurred, utterly sincere): "...is this all on Runware?" VEL turns her head and looks at him. Nothing else moves. Camera: 24 mm, static, low, deep focus, the sky taking two thirds of the frame. Light: starlight, the lantern's ember, no other light. Sound: only the drizzle and seven people breathing, then the one line, then nothing.
```

**After 3F:** fade to black over 1.5 s; `title_render` "Is this all on Runware?" white serif on black, 2 s; end.
