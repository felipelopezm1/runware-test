**Art direction:** two colours of light fighting down one corridor: amber from the front, green from the stairs behind, and a cold grey promise at the archway that grows every shot. Water on the floor doubles every light. The party is a line moving toward the camera and the archway; the frogs are a texture on every surface. The score-keeping is the comedy; 2C kills it in one look.

## 2A. Eleven. Nine. (5 s) — over Mordecai's shoulder
**Frame (references `IMG_8128`, `IMG_8127`, the corridor plate, the prints):** from behind Mordecai's horned helmet and iron shoulder at the top of the stairs, the barrels beside him, looking down the flooded corridor: Borrin's lantern a few metres ahead, Vel and Vessa behind him, frogs on every surface, the bench along the left wall, the archway a grey slot at the far end.
**SHOT body:**
```
0 to 2 s: over MORDECAI's horned helmet and iron-plated shoulder at the top of the stairs, the camera follows him as he steps into the long flooded corridor behind the party's fighting line. He flings one arm out and a ring of acid-green fire rolls out across the water ahead, lighting the weeping walls green and curling a dozen frogs into smoke; he says (relishing it): "Eleven." Ahead, VEL lunges in a straight line and spits a blue frog on her rapier without looking back and says (flat): "Nine." MORDECAI (delighted): "Assists are not kills, Captain." 2 to 5 s: DAX, two bounces on the top step beside him, leaps from the stairs high over the barrels and the line and chops down with his square-ended half sword in both hands onto a dog-sized toad, which bursts milky venom; he lands, his fur boots skid out, and he goes down on his back in the water with a two-handed flourish of the sword, shouting (bright): "Seven!" VESSA, striding past him toward the camera with her shield up and her broken-tipped sword overhead, says (warm): "Six and a half." BORRIN's lantern swings on his axe at the front; OSWIN and SYLWEN are in the middle of the line; frogs cling to the chains overhead. Camera: 35 mm, rear three-quarter, a slow steady follow that keeps Mordecai's shoulder and horns in the left of frame. Light: green fire from his hand, amber lantern ahead, grey at the far archway, every light doubled on the water. Sound: the frog chorus echoing, splashing feet, the hiss of the fire, steel, Dax's skid and splash.
```

## 2B. Amphibians (4 s) — Oswin's hand in frame
**Frame (references `IMG_8130`, `IMG_8124`, Oswin's, Sylwen's, Borrin's and Vessa's prints):** hip height, three-quarter front on Oswin mid-cast, his raised left hand in the foreground wrapped in clear water and blue-white light, the crystal on his staff glowing, a line of frogs on the flooded floor beyond him; Vessa flat on her back among frogs behind her shield in the midground, laughing; Sylwen behind bandaging her hand; Borrin at frame left, lantern high on the axe.
**SHOT body:**
```
0 to 2 s: OSWIN plants his boots in the shallow water, raises his open left hand high and casts: clear water gathers out of the air around his hand and the crystal on his staff with a blue-white glow and launches as a wave two metres wide down the corridor, which rolls over a dozen frogs and toads; they sit in it, shining, and a blood-orange frog croaks at him. He says (reedy, rising, baffled): "They're AMPHIBIANS. They should... I don't understand..." Behind him a dog-sized toad slams into VESSA and knocks her flat on her back among the frogs; she comes up behind her red shield laughing. 2 to 4 s: SYLWEN walks on without stopping, tying a bandage round her own right hand with her teeth and her free hand, and says (even): "That one was yours, Oswin." At the left edge of the frame BORRIN head-butts a toad off the bench with his conical helmet, raises his pewter flask to it as it falls and says (slurred): "To you, big lad." Camera: 50 mm, hip height, handheld float, a slight drift with the party's walk toward the camera. Light: the blue-white glow on Oswin's hand and the wave, lantern amber from the left, a green edge from the stairs behind. Sound: the wave's rush and splash, the orange frog's croak, Oswin's voice echoing, the toad's slam and Vessa's laugh, the thud of the head-butt.
```

## 2C. They're running (6 s)
**Frame (references `IMG_8131`, `IMG_8119`, the corridor plate):** a profile wide of the corridor, the party in a line, Vel at the front stopped, Borrin's lantern beside her, the open archway at the far right with cold grey light and mist beyond, the barred window niche on the wall behind them, frogs streaming toward the left edge of frame past the party's feet back toward the stairs.
**SHOT body:**
```
0 to 2 s: in profile, the camera tracks with the party's line moving right along the flooded corridor and slows as VEL stops dead at the front; the others stop behind her, BORRIN's lantern swinging to a halt on his axe. The last frogs do not attack: they stream past the party's boots toward the left edge of the frame, back toward the stairs, splashing. 2 to 4 s: VEL watches them go and says (quiet, cold): "They're not attacking." She turns her head to the open archway at the far right, cold grey light and a faint mist beyond it, and says: "They're running." BORRIN (quiet for once): "Told you. Pond." MORDECAI, at the back, lifts a flaming hand and says (low, happy): "Oh, good." 4 to 6 s: the camera is now static; VEL walks right to the archway and steps into it and stands as a silhouette against grey starlit mist, her cloak and rapier in outline, the lantern following behind her. The shot ends on that silhouette. Camera: 40 mm, eye level, a parallel track that slows to a lock-off, deep focus. Light: lantern amber at the left, green behind the line, the grey glow at the right growing across the shot. Sound: the chorus fading back down the corridor, water running, boots, a cold wind from the archway, no music.
```
