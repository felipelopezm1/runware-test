# Is this all on Runware?

Captain Vel wants to bring her six bickering companions up out of an abandoned dungeon alive on two days of food, but the swarm of poisonous frogs that floods the storeroom is itself running from what waits above: a gigantic screaming earthworm coiled in the mud of the castle's roofless hall.

## 1A: 1A. The last of the bread

Seven travellers rest in one pool of amber lantern light among crates, barrels and sacks in a vaulted stone cellar; everything outside the pool is black. VEL stands at the centre facing the camera, her cloak hanging, counting rations in a pouch, and does not look up. Left of her BORRIN stands at the foot of the stairs and drinks from his pewter flask, his axe planted beside him with the lit lantern hanging under its head. The worn stairs climb away behind them left of centre; the three iron-banded barrels stand behind Vel to the right. SYLWEN stands against the left wall, eyes closed, arms folded. DAX bounces on his toes at the front left and grins. At the right VESSA stands at a crate and cuts a small loaf on it with a downward stroke of her broken sword. MORDECAI and OSWIN stand in the front of the circle with their backs to the camera: a thin green flame flickers between Mordecai's finger bones; Oswin leans on his crystal staff, his left hand rising. Wet stone shines amber near the lantern; one torch gutters orange beside the far staircase. The camera holds for one second, then pushes in slowly; nothing else moves but the people and the flames.

> **Vessa** (cutting bread on a crate, warm, teasing): That's your half, Borrin. The small one.
> **Borrin** (slurred, cheerful, flask in hand): I had the smaller half last time, lads. I measured.
> **Dax** (bouncing by the foot of the stairs, bright): He measured with his mouth.
> **Vel** (by the crates, not looking up, flat): Two days of food. Keep your voices down.


## 1B: 1B. The dead pond

A held, breathing medium close-up of BORRIN at the foot of the stairs, from beside the barrels at the back of the cellar: the worn steps climb behind him to the right toward the dark doorway, and the three iron-banded barrels stand in the lower right foreground. The lantern hanging from his axe lights his face amber, half lit; the cellar beyond is cold black. BORRIN sniffs twice, lifts his nose and looks up the stairs, and speaks. Focus then racks from his face to the open barrel of black water in the lower right of the frame, where a single ripple crosses the surface, then a second, and the lid of the next barrel rocks once; BORRIN's head snaps round to the barrel. Oswin's murmur and Sylwen's voice are heard from off frame. Faintly, from the dark doorway at the top of the stairs, a wet chirping begins.

> **Oswin** (leaning on his staff, eyes shut, quietly to himself): ...and the waters shall know their own...
> **Borrin** (sniffing toward the barrels, half joking): Smells like a pond in here. A dead pond.
> **Sylwen** (eyes closed, back to the right wall, even): The barrel moved.


## 1C: 1C-1. Company: the stairs (from the doorway)

Low on the top step in the dark doorway at the top of the stairs, looking down the worn stone staircase into the cellar. The first of the frogs hops down the upper steps in the foreground, more pressing behind it. At the foot of the stairs BORRIN looks up; beyond him the party, at rest a moment before, turns toward the stairs: VEL turns round, SYLWEN turns from the left wall, VESSA raises her sword, DAX and OSWIN stare. Across the room MORDECAI raises one hand and acid-green flame flares in his palm and lights the whole staircase green. Then the FROGS AND TOADS pour past the camera and down the stairs toward the party in one wet mass, blue, yellow, orange, violet and lime frogs leaping over warty olive toads, climbing the wall beside the steps, tongues lashing; a frog pops up on a barrel beside the stairs. The camera holds low on the top step. Lantern amber at the foot, the green flare on the steps, black behind the camera.

> **Mordecai** (at the back of the circle, pleased, raising one hand; green flame flares from his palm and lights the stairs): Company.


## 1D: 1C-2. Company: the room erupts (behind Mordecai and Oswin)

Low from the front of the cellar behind MORDECAI and OSWIN, whose backs fill the foreground, looking back across the room to the stairs at the left and the three iron-banded barrels beside their top at the right. The barrel lids burst open and frogs erupt out of the barrels in a spray of black water. BORRIN is already on the top step and raises his axe, the lantern swinging high under its head so the light climbs the walls; VEL is already moving with her rapier level. VESSA chops her broken-tipped sword down into the frogs on the barrels and VEL thrusts her rapier into the frog on the nearer barrel: the first kills; in front of Mordecai and DAX a frog each lies crushed on the floor. The party climbs in a column: Borrin at the top, then Vel, SYLWEN, and Dax leaping up onto the third step mid-column, his fur boots skidding on the wet stone; Oswin and Mordecai follow from the floor, Oswin's left hand raised. VESSA stays by the barrels facing the camera, kisses the broken blade of her sword and raises it overhead with her shield forward: the rear guard. Lantern amber from the top of the stairs, green flame in Mordecai's hand, dead frogs on the barrels and the floor.

> **Vel** (on her feet, rapier out, two words): Lantern up. Line.
> **Vessa** (roaring, delighted, kissing the broken blade, shield up): FINALLY.
> **Dax** (leaping onto the stairs): One!
> **Oswin** (left hand already up, horrified): Oh no. Oh no, no, those are...


## 2A: 2A-1. Eleven. Nine. (over Mordecai's shoulder)

Over MORDECAI's horned helmet and iron-plated shoulder, held in the left of frame, on the lowest steps of the flight inside the corridor's far end, the crate stack with its three barrels beside the foot of the steps at the right of frame: the camera follows him as he steps down into the long flooded corridor behind the party's fighting line. Acid-green fire gathers in a spiral in his raised hand and he throws it: arcs of green fire fly the length of the corridor and land on the front of the swarm, lighting the weeping walls green and curling a dozen frogs into smoke. Ahead, VEL lunges sideways at the two frogs on the long bench at the right of frame and spits a blue frog on her rapier without looking back. VESSA advances down the corridor away from the stairs; BORRIN's lantern swings on his axe at the front of the line; OSWIN and SYLWEN are in the middle of it, backs to the camera; frogs cling to the chains overhead. Green fire from his hand, the amber lantern ahead, grey at the far archway; every light is doubled on the water.

> **Mordecai** (from the top of the stairs, one arm flung out; a ring of green fire rolls down the corridor; counting with relish): Eleven.
> **Vel** (lunging, flat): Nine.
> **Mordecai** (delighted): Assists are not kills, Captain.


## 2B: 2A-2. Seven. Six and a half. (over Mordecai's shoulder)

VESSA strides up past him away from the camera with her shield up and her broken-tipped sword overhead. BORRIN's lantern swings on his axe at the front; OSWIN and SYLWEN are in the middle of the line; frogs cling to the chains overhead. Green light from Mordecai's hand, the amber lantern ahead, grey at the far archway; every light is doubled on the water.

> **Dax** (leaping from the top of the stairs over the barrels onto a toad): Seven!
> **Vessa** (as he skids and lands on his back): Six and a half.


## 2C: 2B. Amphibians (Oswin's hand in frame)

Hip height, close beside OSWIN near the foot of the steps, three-quarter on his face, the long corridor running away past him toward the archway. OSWIN plants his boots in the shallow water, raises his open left hand high and casts: clear water gathers out of the air around his hand and the crystal on his staff with a blue-white glow and launches as a wave two metres wide down the corridor, which rolls over a dozen frogs and toads; they sit in it, shining, and a blood-orange frog croaks at him. Far down the corridor, small and soft, a dog-sized toad slams into VESSA and knocks her flat on her back among the frogs; she comes up behind her red shield laughing. SYLWEN walks past him without stopping, tying a bandage round her own right hand with her teeth and her free hand. At the left edge of the frame, far down by the fallen slab, BORRIN head-butts a toad off the slab with his conical helmet and raises his pewter flask to it as it falls. The blue-white glow on Oswin's hand and the wave, lantern amber from the left, a green edge from the stairs behind.

> **Oswin** (left hand high; a wave rolls down the passage and the frogs sit in it, shining): They're AMPHIBIANS. They should... I don't understand...
> **Sylwen** (bandaging her own hand, walking, even): That one was yours, Oswin.
> **Borrin** (head-butting a toad with his helmet, then raising the flask to it): To you, big lad.


## 2D: 2C. They're running

In profile, the camera tracks with the party's line moving right along the flooded corridor and slows as VEL stops dead at the front; the others stop behind her, BORRIN's lantern swinging to a halt on his axe. The last frogs do not attack: they stream past the party's boots toward the left edge of the frame, back toward the stairs, splashing. VEL watches them go, then turns her head to the open archway at the far right, cold grey light and a faint mist beyond it. MORDECAI, at the back, lifts a flaming hand. The camera is now static. VEL walks right toward the archway, turning a little toward the camera where the near wall ends, steps into the arch, set toward the near side, and stands as a silhouette against grey starlit mist, her cloak and rapier in outline, the lantern following behind her. Lantern amber at the left, green behind the line, the grey glow at the right growing across the shot.

> **Vel** (quiet, cold): They're not attacking.
> **Vel** (looking at the archway and the grey light): They're running.
> **Borrin** (quiet for once): Told you. Pond.
> **Mordecai** (low, happy): Oh, good.


## 3A: 3A. Nobody on the mud

From behind the party, the camera rises slowly from knee height to above their heads as they step out of the dark archway between two stacks of crates into the roofless great hall of a ruined castle under a vast moonless sky thick with stars. A tall curved wall of broken limestone behind the mound and low broken stubs of wall along the long sides, a long stone bench ahead with the last few frogs on it, fallen blocks of the keep at the back right, long wet grass, low mist, and in the centre a mound of rubble sunk in a wide patch of black mud, glossy, bubbling slowly, with old bones and broken barrels half sunk in it, a brown haze above it, and something huge and pale coiled round the mound under the muck. VEL raises one hand and everyone stops. DAX bounces twice on his toes. The coil heaves under the mud and a long ripple runs across it toward the bench; the frogs on the bench leap off it and scatter in every direction, most of them away from the mound, two or three onto it. The party's backs are in silhouette against the lantern and the mud; starlight on the walls, the amber lantern, the green flame at the back of the group.

> **Vel** (one hand up, stopping everyone, watching the mud): Nobody on the mud.
> **Dax** (two bounces on his toes, grinning): Define "on".
> **Sylwen** (even): Borrin. Step back.


## 3B: 3B. The scream (Borrin's POV)

From BORRIN's eyes at the edge of the mud, the lantern hanging lit from his axe in the lower left of the frame, the pale coil round the rubble mound heaving at centre. The mud explodes upward and THE WORM rears out of its coil, a 2 m thick body of translucent pink-grey-brown segmented skin slick with mucus and caked in black mud rising straight up eight metres, pale crescent plates of skin sloughing off it; the camera tilts up with it, shaking, until its blind rounded head is against the stars; the head peels open into a round ringed mouth and it screams; mud rains down on the lens. Left of centre OSWIN's left hand comes up high wrapped in water and blue-white light, and at the far left edge MORDECAI spreads both arms wide with green flame blazing in each palm. VEL's arm sweeps through the frame from the left throwing a clay pot that bursts on the worm's flank in an acid-green hiss and white smoke. Lantern amber from below, green from the left, starlight, the acid flash.

> **Oswin** (praying fast, water gathering at his hand): Lord of the deep water, Lord of the deep water, Lord of the...
> **Mordecai** (over the scream, arms spreading wide, thrilled): Now THAT is worth getting up for.
> **Vel** (throwing a pot; green hiss; hard and clipped, not shouted): Flanks! Vessa, the collar!


## 3C: 3C-1. It's soft: the collar

Handheld, close on VESSA as she hacks at the worm's swollen pale collar with her broken-tipped sword, her red shield up, mud and mucus sliding over her bare tattooed arms; she laughs through her teeth. The thin tail end of the worm pulls out of the coil and sweeps across the ground through the middle of the party: it slams her flat on her back behind her shield in the black mud, and the coil closes round SYLWEN to the waist where she stands at its edge, her arms free above it, her face unchanged. Beyond, soft, frogs leap at DAX and MORDECAI. The camera stays with Vessa from the collar to the ground. Starlight, lantern amber from the right, green flame flaring behind the worm.

> **Vessa** (hacking at the worm's collar as it rears, laughing through her teeth): It's SOFT. Why is it SOFT.


## 3D: 3C-2. It's soft: the heal

Held on the two women at eye level: VESSA flat on her back behind her red shield in the black mud, SYLWEN standing pinned to the waist in the pale coil beside her, her arms free above it. A wave of clear water crashes across the frame from the left and slaps a sheet of mud off the worm's flank behind them. SYLWEN, held in the coil, bends down to VESSA's bleeding left forearm beside her and lays two glowing green palms on it, the wound closing to a pink line; her face does not change. The body coils on behind them, half under the mud. Starlight, lantern amber from the right, green flame flaring behind the worm, Sylwen's soft green pour.

> **Oswin** (astonished at himself): It worked. It WORKED.
> **Sylwen** (from inside the coil, reaching down to Vessa's bleeding arm, green light in her palms, dry): Hold still. Yes, it's soft. Hold still.


## 3E: 3D-1. Eight: the jump (over Vessa's shoulder, the rain begins)

Low, from behind VESSA's shoulder as she pushes herself up out of the mud behind her shield, the camera arcs to follow DAX as he sprints from the party at the coil and runs up the worm's own coiled body as a ramp. The sky opens: cold rain falls in sheets, backlit green by Mordecai's flame. DAX launches from the top of the coil, both hands on his square-ended half sword, three metres into the air, lands on the worm's blind rearing head and drives the half blade in to the crossguard. THE WORM screams and whips upright; his fur boots slip on the slick skin and he slides down its side into the mud. SYLWEN stands pinned in the coil at the left. Green flame through the rain, starlight fading behind cloud, the mud shining.

> **Dax** (launching, both hands on the Halfblade): EIGHT!


## 3F: 3D-2. Eight: the rescue (Nobody. On the mud.)

Low, from waist height behind VESSA as she scrambles forward through the mud, the camera holds on the coil at the left. The coil round SYLWEN loosens as the worm's body writhes. VEL, already in the mud up to her knees, grabs SYLWEN under both arms and hauls her up and out of the loop of the coil, speaking through her teeth toward where Dax fell. Rain hammers the mud flat and runs off everyone. Green flame through the rain, starlight fading behind cloud, the lantern guttering, the mud shining.

> **Vessa** (scrambling up, raw): DAX!
> **Vel** (into the mud, hauling Sylwen out of the coil by both arms, through her teeth, to Dax): I said. Nobody. On the mud.


## 3G: 3E. This one is mine (Vel's POV from the mud)

From VEL's eyes, kneeling in the mud at its edge just behind and below him, the camera looks up through sheets of rain at MORDECAI standing at the mud's edge among the party, his plum tabard streaming, both arms raised high and wide with acid-green flame blazing in each skeletal hand; above and beyond him THE WORM rears to its full height and screams with its round mouth peeled wide open, rings of pale ridges leading into black. A thick beam of acid-green soul-flame fires from both his hands straight down the worm's throat; the camera snap-zooms onto his skull and horned helmet and settles; one bolt of lightning splits the sky behind the walls; the worm's body spasms and the head falls away out of the top of the frame onto the mound. MORDECAI turns his skull down to the lens. The green beam lights the rain and the whole hall green; the lightning is white.

> **Mordecai** (grandly, to Vel, as the mouth opens): Watch closely, Captain. This one is mine.
> **Vel** (setting Sylwen down beside Dax, flat, not looking at Mordecai): Assist.
> **Mordecai** (outraged, delighted): It is NOT an assist.


## 3H: 3F. Is this all on Runware? (smash cut)

A locked-off, low, wide tableau from the edge of the still mud, the dead worm's pale body slumped over the rubble mound behind; the sky takes two thirds of the frame through the roofless walls, thick with stars. The seven of them, soaked and filthy, stand and sit in a loose line looking up: VEL standing, rain running off her chin, rapier point down; MORDECAI with his skull tilted up to the sky, the flame out; VESSA with an arm around DAX, who sits in the mud grinning, winded; BORRIN with the lantern a faint ember on his axe and his flask in his other hand; SYLWEN standing with her hands clasped at her belly again; OSWIN wringing out his long white beard with both hands. Nobody speaks. The rain thins to a drizzle; the mud steams. BORRIN raises the flask to the stars and speaks. VEL turns her head and looks at him. Nothing else moves. Starlight and the lantern's ember are the only light.

> **Borrin** (quiet, slurred, utterly sincere, raising the flask to the stars): ...is this all on Runware?
