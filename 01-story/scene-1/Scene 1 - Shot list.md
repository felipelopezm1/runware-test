**Art direction for the scene:** the lantern on Borrin's axe is the only key; faces are lit from one side, warm, with cold black behind. The staircase is a dark ramp on the right ending in a black doorway, six steps rimmed by the torch. The barrels at the left are the second threat. Composition: the party low in the frame, the vault heavy above, the stairs always right. Colour: amber, black, a thread of green when Mordecai's hand flares. Pace: slow, then a snap.

## 1A. The last of the bread (5 s)
**Frame (Nano Banana Pro; references `IMG_8086`, the seven prints' front views, the storeroom plate):** the master plate with the seven placed as the build: Vessa and Oswin centre left with a loaf on a crate between them, Mordecai centre back, Vel centre by the crates, Dax back right at the foot of the stairs, Sylwen by the right wall, Borrin front right at the bottom step, the lantern on his axe lighting them.
**SHOT body:**
```
0 to 5 s: a slow, delayed push in from the storeroom's open front toward the seven resting in lantern light among crates and barrels. VESSA, sitting on a crate with her shield leaning beside her, cuts a small loaf with a knife and hands BORRIN the smaller piece; he weighs it in his hand and drinks from his pewter flask, his axe planted beside him with the lantern hanging lit under its head. DAX bounces on his toes at the foot of the stairs and grins. VEL stands by the crates with her cloak hanging, counting rations in a pouch, and does not look up. MORDECAI stands at the back of the circle with a thin green flame flickering between his finger bones. SYLWEN stands against the right wall, eyes closed, arms folded. OSWIN leans on his crystal staff, lips moving. Dialogue, low and tired, overlapping: VESSA (warm, teasing): "That's your half, Borrin. The small one." BORRIN (slurred, cheerful): "I had the smaller half last time, lads. I measured." DAX (bright): "He measured with his mouth." VEL (flat, not looking up): "Two days of food. Keep your voices down." Camera: 35 mm, low three-quarter, a slow push that begins after one second; nothing else moves but the people and the flames. Light: one amber lantern at waist height on the axe and one guttering torch beside the far staircase, everything else black. Sound: dripping water with a long echo, the torch hiss, cloth, the knife on bread, quiet voices.
```

## 1B. The dead pond (4 s)
**Frame (references `IMG_8093`, Borrin's and Oswin's prints, the barrels plate):** a chest-height two-shot of Borrin and Oswin, Borrin's nose up toward the barrels at the left, the lantern on the axe between them lighting both faces, the open barrel of black water in the lower left of the frame, Sylwen a soft pale shape at the right wall behind.
**SHOT body:**
```
0 to 2 s: a held, breathing medium close-up of BORRIN and OSWIN lit by the lantern hanging from Borrin's axe. OSWIN, eyes shut, leaning on his staff, murmurs (quiet, old): "...and the waters shall know their own..." BORRIN lifts his nose toward the barrels at the left, sniffs twice, and says (half joking, slurred): "Smells like a pond in here. A dead pond." 2 to 4 s: focus racks from their faces to the open barrel of black water in the lower left of the frame, where a single ripple crosses the surface, then a second, and the lid of the next barrel rocks once. Behind them, soft and out of focus against the right wall, SYLWEN opens her eyes and says (even, quiet): "The barrel moved." Faintly, from the dark doorway at the top of the stairs, a wet chirping begins. Camera: 50 mm, chest height, handheld breath only, no push, one rack of focus. Light: lantern amber from the right, faces half lit, cold black behind. Sound: drips, the two sniffs, the ripple, the lid, the chirping very faint.
```

## 1C. Company (6 s) — Dax's POV
**Frame (references `IMG_8096`, Mordecai's print, the stairs plate):** from the foot of the stairs looking up the staircase to the black doorway: six torch-lit steps, then darkness filled with dozens of small gold eyes, and a green flare from the left edge of frame lighting the lower steps.
**SHOT body:**
```
0 to 1.5 s: from DAX's point of view at the foot of the stairs, looking up the worn stone staircase along the right wall to the black doorway at its top. Dozens of gold eyes with horizontal pupils open in the dark beyond the sixth step. From the left edge of frame MORDECAI's bony hand rises with acid-green flame in the palm and lights the whole staircase green, and he says (pleased, quiet): "Company." 1.5 to 4 s: the FROGS AND TOADS pour down the stairs toward the camera as one wet mass, blue, yellow, orange, violet and lime frogs leaping over warty olive toads, climbing the wall beside the steps, tongues lashing; the camera floats back and swings left across the room as Dax backs off, and behind the party the barrel lids burst open and more frogs erupt out of the barrels in a spray of black water. VEL is already moving with her rapier level and says (two words, flat): "Lantern up. Line." BORRIN raises his axe and the lantern swings high under its head; the light climbs the walls. 4 to 6 s: VESSA kisses the broken blade of her sword, raises it straight overhead with her shield forward and roars (delighted): "FINALLY." DAX's own forearms and square-ended half sword enter the bottom of the frame as he leaps up onto the third step and his fur boots skid on the wet stone, shouting (bright): "One!"; under it OSWIN (old, horrified): "Oh no. Oh no, no, those are..." The frame ends looking up the stairs with the party starting to climb them through the frogs, the dark doorway above. Camera: 28 mm, handheld float, one continuous move, never cuts. Light: lantern amber swinging, green flare on the stairs, torch orange. Sound: the chirping chorus becoming a roar of wet slaps on stone, lids clattering, water, steel drawn, Vessa's roar, boots on wet steps.
```
