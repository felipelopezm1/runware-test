## LOCK BLOCK (paste unchanged)
> STOREROOM: a rectangular vaulted cellar of dark grey ashlar stone, 8 m by 6 m, 5 m to the apex of a ribbed barrel vault, the mortar black with damp, moss and pale lichen in the joints, water running in thin lines down the walls. Along the back-left corner three stacked wooden crates and a slumped sack; at the front left a cluster of three iron-banded barrels and a sack, one barrel's lid off and full of black water. From the floor at the front a straight stone staircase 1.5 m wide climbs along the right wall to a dark doorway in the back-right corner, its steps worn hollow and slick, two more crates and a fallen block at its top. Loose fallen blocks on the floor. The only light is the dwarf's amber lantern and one guttering wall torch beside the stairs; the vault above is black.

## Layout (from the build)
The default camera looks in from the open front of the room. Crates and a sack in the back-left corner; barrels and a sack front left; the staircase runs from the floor at the front right up along the right wall to the back-right corner, crates at its top. The party rests in a loose circle in the middle: Vessa and Oswin centre left (bread on a crate between them), Mordecai centre back with his arms half raised, Vel centre, Dax back right by the foot of the stairs, Sylwen by the right wall, Borrin front right at the bottom step with the lantern. The frogs come first down the stairs from the dark doorway (one, then a stream) and then burst out of the barrels (the blue flame shapes in `IMG_0349` to `0352`): two fronts. The party's way out is UP the stairs into the corridor (the blue arrows up-right in `IMG_0345`).

## Materials and light words (for the plates and `scene_style`)
Dark grey ashlar blocks 60 by 40 cm with black damp mortar, pale green-grey lichen, dripping moss in the vault ribs, puddles with a skin of dust, soot above the torch bracket; oak crates grey with age, iron-banded barrels, coarse sacking. Lantern: warm amber key from waist height on the axe, hard shadows climbing the walls. Torch: a flickering orange rim on the first six steps. Everything else falls to black. Cold blue only in the deepest shadow. Air: faint dust, no fog.

## Sound
Dripping water with a long echo, the torch hiss, the lantern's small crackle, breathing, cloth, a knife on bread; then from the dark doorway above, a wet chirping that grows; then the lid of a barrel rocking.
