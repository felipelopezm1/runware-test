# 01-story: what the film tells

The script, the shot lists and the storyboard: everything that says what happens, in which order, seen how.

| file or folder | what it is | made by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`SCRIPT.md`](SCRIPT.md) | the full script: every line, its delivery, the shot timings | written by hand before the quest |
| [`script.fountain`](script.fountain) | the same script in Fountain, as the storyboard holds it | the agents, from SCRIPT.md |
| [`shotlist.md`](shotlist.md) | the storyboard's shot list (rev 15): logline, the 16 takes with size, angle, move, lens and length, and the full action and dialogue of each | the agents, from the hand-written shot lists and the action maps |
| `storyboard.json` | the storyboard itself, the file the storyboard editor opens (every shot, take, cost and decision) | the agents; copied in at the end |
| [`ambiances/`](ambiances/) | "The three ambiances, one place": the WORLD block and the SCENE 1 to 3 blocks (ambiance, tone, how people speak, sound) and the seams between them | written by hand |
| [`scene-1/`](scene-1/), [`scene-2/`](scene-2/), [`scene-3/`](scene-3/) | per scene, the hand-written direction: `Scene N - Environment.md` and `Scene N - Shot list.md` | written by hand |

The hand-written shot lists planned 12 shots (1A, 1B, 1C, 2A, 2B, 2C, 3A to 3F); the agents' director split and reshaped them into the storyboard's 16 takes, matching the action maps where the maps and the lists disagreed (the master prompt's rule: the map wins, and the change is written in the log). `shotlist.md` is the one that is being filmed.

Scene lengths: 15 s, 15 s, 30 s, plus a 1.5 s fade and a 2 s title card.

**Where the files are:** what is committed is in this folder; everything else is in the [Drive folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1FWxTWA6g5YAU0IvnW3phteEQP4Db5o-Y) under `01-story/`, which mirrors these folders. Nothing here points at a private machine.
